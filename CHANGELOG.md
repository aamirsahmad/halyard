## [0.1.0]

First public release: an unofficial desktop client for Signal on macOS and Linux, linked to
your phone as a secondary device. Messaging only; calls aren't supported yet.

### Added

- **Chats**:
  - 1:1 and group chats, and Note to Self.
  - Replies, reactions, editing, deleting, forwarding and message info.
  - Formatting, @mentions, link previews, stickers, view-once media and attachments.
  - Voice notes: playback, and recording as MP3.
- **Chat list**:
  - Pinning, muting, archiving and marking unread.
  - Search and drafts.
  - Collapses to avatars in narrow windows.
- **Groups and contacts**:
  - Message requests and blocking, synced with your phone.
  - Disappearing messages.
  - Safety numbers.
  - Contact and group photos.
  - Leaving groups, and group membership notices.
- **Stories**: viewing, replying and posting.
- **App**:
  - Light, dark and system themes, and zoom.
  - Notifications and keyboard shortcuts.
  - Encrypted local storage.
- **Downloads**:
  - macOS universal `.dmg` and `.zip`.
  - Linux AppImage, `.deb` and tarball for x86_64 and arm64.
