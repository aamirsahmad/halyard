## [0.1.0]

First release. Halyard is an unofficial Signal client for macOS and Linux. It connects to your
phone as a linked device. It does not support calls.

### Added

- 1:1 chats, group chats and Note to Self, with replies, reactions, edits, deletion and forwarding.
- Formatting, @mentions, link previews, stickers, view-once media, attachments and voice messages.
- Message requests, blocking, disappearing messages, safety numbers and leaving groups.
- Stories: view, reply and post.
- Themes, zoom, notifications, keyboard shortcuts and encrypted local storage.
- Packages: macOS `.dmg` and `.zip`, Linux AppImage, `.deb` and `.tar.gz`.
